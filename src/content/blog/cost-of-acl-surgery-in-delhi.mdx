## Factors Influencing ACL Reconstruction Cost in Delhi

The total financial outlay for knee ligament surgery depends on several clinical and non-clinical variables:

### 1. Choice of Graft Type
The torn ACL must be replaced with a tissue graft. The graft source significantly alters the overall procedure cost:
* **Hamstring Tendon Autograft:** The most widely used approach; cost-effective with high success rates.
* **Patellar Tendon Autograft:** Preferred by high-impact professional athletes; slightly higher equipment costs.
* **Quadriceps Tendon Autograft:** Ideal for revision ACL surgeries or larger patients.
* **Allograft (Donor Tissue):** More expensive due to the cost of processed donor tissue, but eliminates donor-site pain.

### 2. Implant Material & Fixation Hardware
The graft is secured inside the bone tunnels using modern fixation devices:
* **Titanium Screws:** Durable and cost-effective.
* **Bioabsorbable/Bio-composite Screws:** These dissolve gradually into the bone over time, reducing future hardware issues (slightly higher cost).
* **Cortical Button / Adjustable Loop Devices:** State-of-the-art suspensory fixation used for high primary stability.

### 3. Hospital Category & Room Choice
* **Hospital Accreditation:** Top-tier tertiary care institutions (e.g., Max Healthcare, Apollo Hospitals, Fortis, Manipal) charge higher room and nursing fees compared to standalone nursing homes.
* **Room Selection:** Costs vary based on your selection of a General Ward, Semi-Private Room, Private Deluxe Room, or Suite.

### 4. Surgeon’s Expertise
Senior orthopedic knee specialists with fellowship training in arthroscopy and sports medicine often charge higher surgical fees. However, choosing an experienced surgeon drastically reduces the risk of graft failure or postoperative complications.

### 5. Concomitant Injuries (Meniscus Repair)
Many ACL tears are accompanied by a **meniscal tear** or collateral ligament (MCL/LCL) injury. If a meniscus repair (stitching) or meniscectomy (trimming) is required alongside the ACL reconstruction, the cost may increase by **₹25,000 to ₹50,000**.

## Recovery and Rehabilitation Timeline

Recovery from an ACL reconstruction requires patience and dedicated physiotherapy. Here is a general timeline of what to expect:

* **Days 1 – 7:** Resting, managing swelling with ice and compression, performing basic ankle pumps, and walking with crutches and a knee brace.
* **Weeks 2 – 6:** Gradual full weight-bearing, restoring full knee extension, and achieving 90 to 120 degrees of knee flexion.
* **Months 2 – 4:** Muscle strengthening (quadriceps and hamstrings), balance exercises, and stationary cycling.
* **Months 4 – 6:** Straight-line running, light agility drills, and sport-specific training.
* **Months 6 – 9+:** Return to competitive non-contact and contact sports (subject to functional clearance by your orthopedic surgeon).
