# The Complete Guide to LASIK Eye Surgery in India (2026)

If you're tired of waking up and immediately reaching for your glasses, or dealing with dry, irritated eyes from contact lenses, you're not alone. Every year, thousands of people across India choose LASIK to regain perfect 6/6 vision. 

In this comprehensive guide, we'll break down everything you need to know about getting LASIK in India in 2026.

## What is Modern Bladeless LASIK?

Traditional LASIK used a microkeratome (a tiny blade) to create the corneal flap. However, top eye hospitals in India now almost exclusively use **Femto-LASIK (Bladeless LASIK)** or **SMILE**. 

These advanced procedures use high-precision lasers to reshape the cornea, making the procedure:
- **100% Painless** (numbing eye drops are used)
- **Extremely Fast** (the actual laser time is under 30 seconds per eye)
- **Safer** (eliminates blade-related complications)

## Who is a Good Candidate?

You might be the perfect candidate for LASIK if you meet these criteria:
1. You are at least 18 years old (ideally 21+ for stable vision).
2. Your eye prescription has been stable for the last 12-18 months.
3. You have sufficient corneal thickness (this will be tested during your pre-evaluation).
4. You don't have underlying eye conditions like severe dry eyes or glaucoma.

## How Much Does LASIK Cost in India?

The cost of LASIK varies depending on the technology used and the city you are in. However, you can expect the following ranges at premium, NABH-accredited hospitals:

- **Standard LASIK:** ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 (both eyes)
- **Femto (Bladeless) LASIK:** ₹60,000 to ₹90,000 (both eyes)
- **SMILE (Flapless):** ₹90,000 to ₹1,20,000 (both eyes)

## The Recovery Process

One of the best parts about modern LASIK is the rapid recovery. 
- **First 24 hours:** Your eyes may feel slightly scratchy or watery. You should rest and keep your eyes closed as much as possible.
- **Day 2:** Most patients experience 6/6 vision the very next morning! You can return to light work.
- **Week 1-2:** You'll need to wear protective sunglasses outdoors, avoid swimming, and strictly use the prescribed antibiotic and lubricating eye drops.
