Dear Yamini,

On behalf of HEALTHEXPRESS INDIA, we are pleased to offer you the position of Case Manager & Patient Care Coordinator. We were thoroughly impressed with your communication skills, dedication to patient welfare, and alignment with our mission to deliver seamless, transparent, and high-quality surgical care to patients across India.

This letter formalizes the terms and conditions governing your employment with HealthExpress India:

1. Commencement of Employment & Location

Your official date of commencement of employment will be 27th January 2026. You shall discharge your duties in a Remote / Work From Home (WFH) capacity, reporting digitally to the Operations Lead / Director. You are expected to maintain an active, high-speed broadband connection and dedicated quiet workstation to handle patient calls and coordinate surgery admissions seamlessly.

2. Compensation & Statutory Structure (MSME Framework)

Your annual Cost to Company (CTC) will be INR 6,20,000/- (Six Lakhs Twenty Thousand Rupees Only), payable in equal monthly installments of INR 51,667/- (Fifty-One Thousand Six Hundred Sixty-Seven Rupees Only), subject to statutory deductions (such as TDS) as applicable under the Indian Income Tax Act.

Statutory Exemption Clarification: As HealthExpress India is a registered Micro-Enterprise under the Ministry of MSME, Govt. of India (Udyam: UDYAM-OD-17-0050610) with an employee headcount under 20, the establishment is statutorily exempt from Employee Provident Fund (EPF) and ESIC deductions under Section 1(3) of the EPF & MP Act, 1952. Consequently, zero PF is deducted, providing you with maximum monthly direct in-hand liquidity. A detailed salary annexure is provided in Annexure A.

3. Probation Period & Confirmation

You will be on probation for an initial period of 3 (Three) Months commencing from your joining date. During probation, your performance, responsiveness, and patient handling outcomes will be evaluated on a continuous basis. Upon satisfactory performance review, your services will be confirmed in writing.

4. Roles, Responsibilities & Code of Conduct

As Case Manager, your primary responsibilities will include:

  • Active coordination with inbound patients inquiring for elective and specialized surgical procedures.
  • Liaising with empanelled hospital partners, administrative coordinators, and surgeons for OPD consultations and surgery admissions.
  • Diligent record-keeping and real-time status updates on the HealthExpress CRM platform.
  • Providing compassionate, professional, and ethical guidance to patients and their families at every step.

5. Confidentiality & Patient Data Privacy (HIPAA / DPDP Act)

In the course of your duties, you will have access to sensitive Patient Health Information (PHI), medical reports, surgery quotations, doctor schedules, and proprietary business processes. You agree to hold all such information in strict confidence and shall not disclose, duplicate, or transmit any patient records or trade secrets to any third party outside authorized care protocols. Strict non-disclosure obligations shall survive the termination of your employment.

6. Working Hours & Exclusivity (Non-Moonlighting)

Your regular working hours will be from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM (Monday through Saturday), with assigned rotational coverage for urgent patient queries. This position is a full-time, exclusive engagement. You shall not engage in any other business, advisory role, or concurrent employment during your association with HealthExpress India.

7. Leave Policy

You will be entitled to 1.5 days of paid leave per month (18 days annually) upon completion of your probation, comprising Casual, Sick, and Earned Leave, in addition to declared national and festival holidays. Prior approval from management is required for planned leaves.

8. Separation & Notice Period

During the probation period, either party may terminate the employment relationship by providing 15 (Fifteen) days' prior written notice or gross salary in lieu thereof. Following successful confirmation, the notice period shall be 30 (Thirty) days. The company reserves the right to terminate employment immediately without notice in cases of proven breach of confidentiality, gross misconduct, or ethical violations.
